RALPH COURT

More possibility within a smaller space.

Ralph Court is a studio apartment that required renovation. To maximise the limited space, we incorporated a Murphy bed into minimalist timber cabinetry, allowing it to be easily pulled down when needed and neatly concealed when not in use.
