Falafel Times

Falafel Times is more than just a falafel chain, it’s a celebration of falafel as a symbol of Arab heritage, reclaiming its authentic roots and cultural significance.

The restaurant honors the rich traditions of the region while addressing modern-day challenges, offering a space that seamlessly blends food, history, and hope.

Set in a hypothetical peaceful future, Falafel Times envisions a world where the headlines in the Levant speak of harmony, progress, and unity. Free from external interventions and influences, this future celebrates the identity, culture, and spirit of the region.

While falafel may have its origins in the broader Middle East or Egypt, the concept asserts that falafel belongs to this region, a cherished part of its past, present, and future.

The walls are adorned with newspapers featuring “good news” from this imagined time, providing an inspiring contrast to the struggles faced by the region today.

Through its menu and ambiance, the project invites diners to savor authentic flavors while reflecting on the resilience and stories of the Levant, creating a unique culinary and cultural experience that tastes like hope.
