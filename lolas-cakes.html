Lola’s Cakes

A digital interface concept for a cupcake shop, moving from hand-drawn ideas to a desktop shopping experience.

UX/UI course design concept. This portfolio prototype is not a live shop and does not accept real orders or payments.

Early exploration

From first browse to delivery

The sketches explore product browsing, allergen filters and cake customisation, alongside short preparation, delivery and thank-you scenes. These are selected moments from a proposed ordering experience, rather than a complete checkout flow.

Ordering storyboard

  1. 01

    Discover the shop

    A cake-decorating video introduces the shop and its personality.

  2. 02

    Browse and filter

    A product grid and allergen filters help someone explore the menu.

  3. 03

    Make it personal

    Customisation controls explore size, flavours and decoration.

  4. 04

    Prepare the order

    A playful preparation scene carries the brand into the waiting moment.

  5. 05

    Follow the delivery

    An order-on-the-way scene gives delivery its own place in the experience.

  6. 06

    Close with a thank-you

    A final cake video ends the proposed sequence with a warm thank-you.

Developing the layouts

The layout studies separate browsing from building a cake, with filters on the menu and grouped choices for customisation. The storyboard explores how cake-making imagery could carry the shop’s personality into the interface.
