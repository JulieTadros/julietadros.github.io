An exploratory feature for the WomenBank app described in the course brief. The brief focused on convenient payments abroad. Informal interviews described other frustrations too: exchanging cash, comparing rates and resolving card problems while travelling.

From payments to currency exchange

I explored a virtual currency card that lets someone compare providers, add funds and switch currencies. An instant-help route responded to the difficulty of contacting a bank from abroad.

What I learned

The initial brief did not capture every concern. Listening to people changed the direction of the concept. The flow remains exploratory: rate transparency, confirmation and recovery from errors would need testing, alongside a review of technical feasibility.
