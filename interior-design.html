Interior design & architecture

Selected residential, hospitality and retail projects. My work spans concept development, spatial planning, materiality, bespoke joinery and FF&E.

Bauhaus penthouse renovation

A renovation and extension in the Middle East, combining clean architectural lines with natural materials. I led the concept, spatial planning, interior architecture, bespoke joinery, materials, lighting and FF&E.

Castle gatehouse

Design development of a selected student proposal at a historic charity foundation, under the project director’s guidance. My work included traditional-construction research, architectural detailing, joinery and materials. The visualisation was revisited independently for the portfolio.

Ralph Court, London

A compact studio organised around full-height storage and a concealed Murphy bed. I developed the interior concept, layout, bespoke joinery, finishes, lighting and FF&E.

Cultural centre: café & food store

A warm, grounded café and retail space combining clay plaster, light timber and patterned limestone. The layout brings efficient service, social seating and local food displays into one coherent interior.

Barcelona apartments

Residential interiors shaped by Moroccan material culture and desert landscapes. Natural fibres, warm tones and custom pieces give each apartment a distinct atmosphere.

Retail & wellness concepts

Independent concepts for a cinnamon café and a yoga studio, exploring natural materials, soft forms and calm spaces for gathering and movement.

Professional collaborations

Selected work in retail and hospitality, including a flexible furniture showroom and a hotel-room refurbishment layout. The original sheets distinguish my contribution from reference imagery.
